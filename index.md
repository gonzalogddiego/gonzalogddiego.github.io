## About Me

I am a **Marie-Curie Fellow** at the **Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)** in the School of Telecommunication Engineering. 

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the **Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences (NYU)** working with Prof. Georg Stadler on sea ice dynamics and discrete element modeling (DEM) as part of the *Sea Ice MURI* project. 

I completed my PhD at the **Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford** under the supervision of Prof. Ian Hewitt and Prof. Patrick Farrell, focusing on viscous contact problems and free-boundary modeling in glaciology.

### Research Interests
* **Computational Fluid Dynamics & Glaciology:** Marine ice sheet dynamics, subglacial cavity formation, sea ice rheology.
* **Numerical Analysis & PDE Constrained Optimization:** Finite element methods, variational inequalities, discrete element models (DEM).
* **Data-driven & Learned Rheologies:** Inferring continuum model parameters from particle scale dynamics.
